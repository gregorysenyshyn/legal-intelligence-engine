# Draft Neutrality & Admissibility Auditor (`draft-neutrality-auditor`)

Use this skill whenever a user asks to review, critique, or tighten a draft affidavit (Form 14A), conference brief (Form 17A/17C), Offer to Settle (Rule 18), or written communication.

---

## Mandatory 5-Point Admissibility & Neutrality Checklist

1. **Eliminate Argument, Opinion, and Emotional Adjectives (Family Law Rules Compliance):**
   - Affidavits must contain only admissible facts within the deponent's personal knowledge, not argument or emotional characterizations.
   - Strip out subjective adjectives (*"narcissistic," "hostile," "manipulative," "unhinged"*). Replace with objective timestamps, verbatim quotes, and direct observations.
2. **Distinguish Direct Observation from Hearsay:**
   - Flag any out-of-court statements offered for the truth of their contents (hearsay) and re-anchor paragraphs in what the deponent **directly saw, heard, or did**, supported by contemporaneous records or third-party exhibits (school records, police occurrence reports, tax returns).
3. **Every Factual Assertion Must Have a Pinpoint Citation:**
   - Verify every date and dollar figure against the SQLite FTS5 index (`./firm search` / `./firm page`) and attach an exact `[Document Name, Page X]` citation.
4. **Child-Centered Logistics First:**
   - Ensure parenting proposals lead with practical, child-centered stability (school-bell transitions, commute minimization, developmental routine) and deterministic Child Support Guidelines math (`./firm calc` / `./firm holidays`).
5. **Clear, Actionable Relief Sought:**
   - Conclude every brief or motion review with a concise, 1-to-3 sentence summary of the exact order sought so the relief requested is unambiguous.
