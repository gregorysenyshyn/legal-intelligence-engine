## 5. Governing Statutory Authorities

1. **Child Support Guidelines, Section 9 (Shared Parenting):**  
   Both parents have physical custody of Tommy at least 40% of the time, triggering two-way set-off support.
2. **Imputing Income (*Drygala v. Pauli*, 2002 CanLII 41868 ON CA):**  
   Mother's unexplained drop from $64,200 to $38,000 constitutes intentional under-employment without reasonable excuse. Court must impute earning capacity of $64,200.00.
3. **Proof of Section 7 Expenses (*Park v. Thompson*, 2005 CanLII 14132 ON CA):**  
   Extraordinary expenses cannot be awarded on speculative claims; third-party verification is mandatory.
4. **Offer to Settle Costs Presumption (*Family Law Rules*, Rule 18(14)):**  
   Father's Offer to Settle of $305.00/mo is more favourable to Mother than the $265.32/mo judicial calculation, entitling Father to Rule 18 costs from the date of the offer.
